## English

Atlas Go is a travel-planning app for exploring attractions and building itineraries in China. The current version does not require an account, include advertising, or track users across apps and websites.

### Information used to provide routes

When a user requests a route, Atlas Go sends the coordinates of the selected attractions, the city, and the selected transport mode to the Atlas Go routing service. The service forwards the route request to Amap to obtain distance and estimated travel time. The current version does not request or transmit the user's live device location.

### Third-party content and services

Attraction images are loaded from openly licensed sources such as Wikimedia Commons and Flickr. Creator, source-page, and license information is displayed in the app. Maps and route results are provided by third-party map services. These providers may receive standard technical information, such as an IP address, when content is requested.

### Accounts, advertising, and analytics

The current version creates no user account, contains no advertising SDK, and uses no third-party analytics SDK. If these practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the relevant version is released.

### Retention and user choices

Atlas Go does not maintain a personal account profile in the current version. Users may stop using or uninstall the app at any time. Questions about privacy can be sent to [borissun0424@icloud.com](mailto:borissun0424@icloud.com) or through the [Support page]({{ '/support/' | relative_url }}).

## 中文

Atlas Go 是一款用于浏览中国景点和生成旅行行程的应用。当前版本不要求注册账号，不接入广告，也不进行跨 App 或跨网站追踪。

### 路线服务使用的信息

当用户请求路线时，Atlas Go 会将所选景点的坐标、城市和交通方式发送给 Atlas Go 路线服务。该服务会向高德地图请求路线距离和预计时间。当前版本不请求或传输用户设备的实时位置。

### 第三方内容与服务

景点图片来自 Wikimedia Commons、Flickr 等公开授权来源，App 会显示作者、原始作品页和许可证信息。地图和路线结果由第三方地图服务提供。请求这些内容时，服务商可能会收到 IP 地址等常规技术信息。

### 账号、广告与分析

当前版本不创建用户账号，不包含广告 SDK，也不使用第三方分析 SDK。如果这些做法发生变化，我们会在发布相关版本前更新本政策和 App Store 隐私声明。

### 保留时间与用户选择

当前版本不维护个人账号资料。用户可随时停止使用或卸载 App。如对隐私有疑问，请发送邮件至 [borissun0424@icloud.com](mailto:borissun0424@icloud.com)，或通过[用户支持页]({{ '/support/' | relative_url }})联系我们。
